The Emotional Reality

The supply chain doesn't care about your paycheck.

The shippers don't care. The brokers don't care. Nobody in this chain gives a damn if you make money or go broke. The shipper just wants freight moved fast and cheap — and they use brokers so they never have to deal with you directly.

That gap is exactly where I live. I'm not here to move you for a quick cut. I put real effort into the quality of every move — someone who slows down enough to look out for your position, both financially and mentally.
